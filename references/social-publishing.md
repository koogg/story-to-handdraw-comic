# Social publishing design

Design for a phone screen first. Platform rules change, so treat the values below as production defaults and verify against the platform UI when publishing.

Keep the selected content form. For single-graphic, apply ratio, safe area, contrast, and lettering guidance only: one undivided image, no panels, swipe sequence, or extra cover by default. A platform destination alone never expands an agreed one-image output.

## Shared master pages

- Default cross-platform comic page: portrait `3:4`, preferably `1080 × 1440` or a larger proportional master.
- Keep faces, balloons, and essential actions away from the outer 6% on the left/right and 7% on the top/bottom.
- For comic pages, use 3–6 substantial panels as a guideline. Reaction strips may add micro-panels, but body text must remain comfortably readable without zooming. Single-graphic has no panels.
- Use strong value contrast behind text and generous padding; maintain consistent gutters for comics only. Do not place critical copy over visually noisy detail.
- Each carousel page needs a clear entry point and a visual reason to swipe: unresolved action, question, changing state, or page-to-page motif.

## Xiaohongshu

- Use `3:4` as the default content and cover ratio; the official open-platform documentation recognizes both `1:1` and `3:4` image ratios. A practical high-resolution master is `1080 × 1440`.
- For a multi-image post, make page 1 a cover-story hybrid when useful: one strong subject, a short hook, and a preview of the visual premise. Avoid a generic title pasted over a busy comic.
- Keep the cover hook short, normally within 6–14 Chinese characters. The first two seconds should communicate genre, conflict, or curiosity.
- Use consistent page numbering or a recurring corner marker only when there are several pages; keep it secondary.

Reference: [Xiaohongshu Open Platform image ratios](https://school.xiaohongshu.com/en/open/product/create-spl-item.html).

## WeChat Official Account

- Use portrait comic pages inside the article body. A `3:4` master works well for phone reading and can be reused on other platforms.
- Create the article cover as a separate derivative, not a crop of a text-heavy comic page. Default head-cover ratio: `2.35:1`, commonly produced at `900 × 383` or a larger proportional size.
- Keep the cover's core face/object and short hook inside the central square-safe region because shares and secondary placements may crop toward `1:1`.
- For a sequence in the article body, add modest breathing space between pages and avoid repeating a large title on every page.

Reference: [WeChat cover production sizes](https://www.canva.cn/sizes/wechat-official-account/). Verify final cropping in the WeChat publishing preview because display behavior can change.

## Platform package

When the user names a platform, honor the selected form and output count. Single-graphic returns its one finished image. For comic series or an explicitly requested publishing package, include useful derivatives within that scope:

- Xiaohongshu: portrait cover/content sequence and suggested reading order.
- WeChat: portrait body page(s), a separate wide head cover when requested or clearly useful, and a square-safe crop note.
- Both: create one 3:4 master sequence, then generate or crop platform-specific covers without changing the comic's story.

Do not promise that an image-generation model can place long Chinese typography perfectly. Inspect all large hooks and essential dialogue. Minor punctuation variation is not a reason to regenerate an otherwise publishable page.
